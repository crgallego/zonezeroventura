Official Zone Zero resources for Ventura County

The primary sources: statutes, hazard maps, both fire authorities, and ember science. When our pages and a fresh official source ever disagree, the official source wins.

Quick answer: The authoritative sources on Zone 0 are the Board of Forestry (regulatory text), CAL FIRE (hazard maps and the ember-resistant zone FAQ), the Ventura County Fire Department and the Ventura City Fire Department (local enforcement), and IBHS (the ember research behind the rules).

Primary sources

Where the rules actually live

  • Board of Forestry and Fire Protection (bof.fire.ca.gov): the Zone 0 regulatory drafts, including the April 17, 2026 text.
  • CAL FIRE (fire.ca.gov): Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps, the official Zone 0 FAQ, and defensible space guidance. The 2025 county remap lives here.
  • OSFM FHSZ viewer: look up your exact parcel's tier, the decisive check in a county where 59,000 of 86,000 mapped parcels are now Very High.
  • Ventura County Fire Department (vcfd.org): the AHJ for the contract cities and unincorporated communities.
  • Ventura City Fire Department: the independent AHJ inside the City of Ventura, with the city's own hazard-mapping process.
  • IBHS (ibhs.org): the ember-intrusion research underlying the ember-resistant zone standard.
  • Statewide context: Zone Zero California, the network's statewide guide and zone checker.

Regulations move. Final Zone Zero rules are expected in late 2026, and local adoptions continue. Anything time-sensitive on this site should be verified against these sources before you make decisions.
