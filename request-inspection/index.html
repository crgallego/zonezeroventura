Request a Zone 0 inspection in Ventura County

Tell us where your property is.

Quick answer: Zone Zero work in the 0 to 5 foot ember-resistant zone often involves fencing, gates, hardscape, and vegetation changes. Hired fence work at $1,000 or more in labor and materials, a permit, or hired workers typically requires a CSLB-licensed contractor (Class C-13 for fencing); owner-builder and minor-work exemptions can apply.

What happens after you submit

1. We check your parcel. Your address is matched against CAL FIRE's 2025 Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps and your community's adopted local map. With 59,000 of the county's 86,000 mapped parcels now Very High, the answer is yes more often than most homeowners expect, and a parcel-level check beats a guess.

2. You get a plain-English rundown. Which tier you're in, which Zone 0 requirements apply, what your fire authority (VCFD or the Ventura City Fire Department) will look for, and what is Required now versus Proposed — not in force versus Recommended now for you.
